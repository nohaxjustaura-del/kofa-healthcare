# KOFA Healthcare

KOFA Healthcare is a U.S.-based evergreen medtech investment fund founded in 2016 in West Lafayette, Indiana. The site introduces the firm to medical device founders, investment partners, and other professional visitors and gives them a direct way to contact KOFA.

## Approved sources

The user's September 15 Word document supplies the copy. Jing Lin's email specifies the information architecture and amendments. The user's follow-ups explicitly request much closer adherence to OrbiMed's style and layout, authorize suitable implementation resources, and establish original medical photography as the latest visual direction.

## Visitor goals

Understand KOFA's early- to mid-stage medical device investment focus, review the portfolio, learn how the firm works and who is involved, and contact the team.

## Required structure

The homepage contains Home, Portfolio, Focus Areas, and Contact only. About, Our Approach, Team, and News are separate navigation destinations. Focus Areas and Portfolio also have dedicated pages. News combines KOFA News and Portfolio News with filters.

Only Alydia Health and Aria CV are Exits. The other fourteen portfolio companies are Active. Team order is Renee Masi, Senior Managing Director; Jing Lin, Managing Director; Dr. Meng Deng, Managing Director. Renee and Meng have explicitly pending biographies and portraits; Jing's existing biography remains.

## Brand commitments

Use the supplied KOFA red artwork prominently. The requested visual reference is https://www.orbimed.com/ and should govern composition, type, proportions, navigation, and photography placement. The homepage hero uses an original clinician collaboration photograph with people composed on the right and readable white copy on a dark left field. Original supporting photographs may show medical-device research and healthy longevity in restrained circular crops. The site should feel clinically credible, founder-friendly, and quietly institutional.

## Constraints

No invented metrics, investment claims, bios, or portfolio stories. No homepage Our Conviction or Portfolio Stories sections. Google controls sitelinks; use crawlable pages and clear navigation without guaranteeing search appearance. Changes are made in the local Next.js project; deployment has not been requested.

## Stack

Next.js 15, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4. Existing company logos and approved source text are retained. Public component code may be adapted after review and with provenance recorded.
