About page:
One Fund. A Long Horizon. An Enduring Commitment.
Founded in 2016 in West Lafayette, Indiana, KOFA Healthcare is a U.S.-based medtech fund investing globally in transformative medical devices.
With a presence in Boston and the San Francisco Bay Area, our investment team is supported by an accomplished network of clinical advisors and industry experts.
As an evergreen fund backed by a family office, we are not constrained by traditional fundraising cycles or predetermined exit timelines. We invest based on conviction in the technology, clinical need and management team—and remain committed for the journey ahead.
Our Approach/ How we invest
We Invest When Conviction Matters Most
Backed by permanent family-office capital, we take a patient, founder-aligned approach—investing in exceptional technologies, clinical needs and teams rather than fixed fund timelines.
We partner early, often before the technology or regulatory pathway is fully de-risked. We look for clinical evidence that supports the mechanism of action and establishes a credible path forward.
Beyond capital, we help founders shape clinical strategy, design first-in-human studies, engage with the FDA and build strong investor syndicates. Our medtech operating experience and U.S.–China network can also connect portfolio companies with clinical, manufacturing and commercial partners.
