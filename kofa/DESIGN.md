## Tokens

- Supporting surface: pale ice `#edf6f7`.
- Main ink: navy `#12394b`; body ink: slate `#4d626c`.
- Accent: teal `#008979`, with a darker accessible foreground variant for small text.
- Hero surface: deep navy `#073449` with white foreground.
- Brand red: supplied logo artwork, unchanged.
- Typography: clean sans-serif throughout; existing Geist is self-hosted. Display 40–60px, weight 400, generous 1.1–1.2 line height. Section titles around 32–42px. Body 16–20px, line height at least 1.55. No decorative serif, exaggerated tracking, or tiny navigation.
- Layout: centered content about 1200px wide, 24px mobile gutters, 40–64px desktop gutters; section spacing roughly 72–104px.
- Rules: subtle cool gray. Cards use one quiet border or a very soft shadow, not both. Buttons have modest radii and visible focus.

## Composition

A solid white header carries a large KOFA symbol and wordmark with straightforward navigation. The first viewport has a full-width clinician photograph with people concentrated on the right. A dark left field supports calm left-aligned white source copy and must remain strong enough at every breakpoint that text never crosses a face. A shallow white curved edge and fine teal contour connect it to the portfolio section.

The portfolio uses Active and Exits as primary controls and a disciplined grid of real company logos and names. A large circular medical-device research photograph supports its introduction without changing the section's copy or controls. Focus areas use the three supplied names and descriptions with restrained technical line icons, plus one circular healthy-longevity photograph near the introduction. Photographs use responsive object positioning and preserve their subjects at phone and desktop sizes. Contact closes the homepage with the supplied invitation in a confident, uncluttered band. Interior pages share the same sans-serif scale and clear content layouts.

## Interaction and accessibility

Keep headings and content visible in server HTML. Remove masking, arrival curtains, scroll-dependent reveals, and ornamental progress rails from the active experience. Respect reduced motion. Tabs support keyboard navigation, selected state, and panel associations. Portfolio filters on the homepage operate in place. Navigation must fit at desktop widths and offer a usable mobile menu.
